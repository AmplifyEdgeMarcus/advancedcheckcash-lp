Borrow wisely

Is a title loan right for you?

A title loan can help in a pinch, but it’s a serious decision. Here’s an honest look.

It may fit if…

  • You have a one-time expense, like a car repair or a medical bill
  • You own your vehicle outright and have the title
  • You have steady income and a clear plan to repay on schedule
  • You’ve compared the total cost with your other options

Think it through if…

  • Title loans are high-cost credit and usually cost more than a bank or credit union loan
  • Your car secures the loan. If you can’t repay, you could lose it
  • You rely on your car for work and have no backup way to get there
  • You’d need to borrow again to cover ongoing bills

Worth checking first: a small loan from a local credit union, a payment plan with the company you owe, or help from family. If a title loan is still the best choice, borrow only what you need.

Pay early, no penalty

Tennessee law doesn’t allow prepayment penalties, so paying off early can save you money.

Tenn. Code §45-15-115(12)

A $2,500 limit

Tennessee caps title loans at $2,500 per vehicle title with the same lender.

Tenn. Code §45-15-115(3)

Common questions

How much can I borrow?

From $100 to $2,500. The amount depends on your vehicle’s value and your ability to repay. Not everyone qualifies, and all loans are subject to approval.

Do I keep my car?

Yes. You keep driving your car while you repay. We hold your title until the loan is paid off.

What do I need to bring?

Your vehicle, a clear title in your name with no other liens, a valid photo ID, and proof of income. You must be at least 18.

Repayment terms range from [RAY: min–max repayment period]. APRs range from [RAY: APR range]. Before you sign, you’ll get a written disclosure showing the APR, every fee, your payment schedule, and the total you’ll repay. See the representative example.

Can I pay it off early?

Yes, any time. There’s no prepayment penalty, and paying early can lower what you pay overall.

What if I can’t make a payment?

Call or text us as early as you can, before your due date. We’ll go over your account and talk through what’s possible. Please understand that if a loan goes into default, we may repossess your vehicle as allowed by Tennessee law.

I live in North Georgia. Can I still come in?

Yes. Many of our customers drive in from Rossville, Fort Oglethorpe, and nearby towns. All loans are made in person at our Tennessee stores under Tennessee law. We don’t have a Georgia office.
